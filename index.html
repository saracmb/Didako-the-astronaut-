DIDAKO

THE ASTRONAUT
Somewhere far out in space… Didako is stuck in his capsule and Houston isn't answering the phone. Help him get home!
1THE MOON. Didako is lost behind the Moon. Touch and hold where you want to fly: left or right to go around the Moon, up or down to go higher or lower. Follow the Earth signal until Earth rises.
2SPACE. Aim for Barcelona! Slide your finger to keep the red target in the crosshair. Dodge rocks & junk, grab ★ and 🔧.
3SPLASHDOWN. The parachute opens! Touch left or right to steer (touch above Didako to slow down). Follow the red arrow and land on the rescue boat in front of Barcelona.
On your own website you can press TILT and steer by tilting the phone or iPad. (Inside the Claude app, use touch.)
